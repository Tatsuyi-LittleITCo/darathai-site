About Us

Dara Thai Dancing School is a vibrant community dance studio dedicated to sharing the beauty, grace, and tradition of Thai dance. We teach students of all ages, from children to adults, and focus on both classical and cultural performance techniques.:contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

Our classes encourage creativity, confidence, and connection to Thai culture and community. We also participate in local festivals and events throughout Perth.

Classes

  • Traditional Thai Dance — Ballet‑style Thai dance fundamentals
  • Folk & Cultural Dance — Rhythmic, energetic Thai folk styles
  • Performance Workshops — Stage performance and choreography

All levels welcome. Contact us for schedules and enrolment.
